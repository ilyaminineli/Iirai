02

Growth principle

Keep the beginning

The original voice remains part of the family and keeps its own release history.

Let the voice change

New generations can become clearer, more expressive, more feminine and more queer as the project and its creator develop.

Make room for more

Appends, extras and new recording techniques expand what the voice can express.

Keep the history visible

Every stage stays documented so the evolution can be heard and understood rather than flattened into one version.

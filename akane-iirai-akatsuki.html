A voice continuing to become itself

暁 is the next stage of the same voicebank after years of recording, editing, performing and changing.

The real voice provider, Ilya Minin (Eli), has come to understand himself as non-binary and is consciously finding a place between genders, moving away from the masculine origin that no longer feels representative. The voicebank's evolution records that process artistically.

Compared with the original イーライ, the line has gradually become softer, more expressive and less tied to fixed presentation. 茜音イーライ made that movement more visible; 暁 intentionally takes it further.

03

A voice continuing to become itself

暁 is the third released generation of the same voicebank line. It keeps the history of イーライ and 茜音イーライ visible while expanding the recording and expressive vocabulary of the project.

The progression from イーライ to 茜音イーライ and then to 暁 is treated as a record of changing self-expression rather than a reset of the character.

同じ声の、次のかたち。
Another form of the same voice.

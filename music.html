Growing archive

The voice changes. The archive remembers.

Each work documents a moment in the development of the voicebank: from the original イーライ form through the more feminine and queer 茜音イーライ and toward the still-developing 茜音イーライ・暁.

Works using 暁 will be added as the third-generation recordings become public.

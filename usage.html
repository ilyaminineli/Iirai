茜音イーライ

Japanese CVVC, Romaji-encoded and CVVC-aliased. The released bank contains A3, F3 and C3 pitches and is most comfortable around 70–120 BPM.

Resamplers & expression

TIPS is the primary recommendation when preserving the warm bass character is important. Moresampler provides the documented expression controls; WORLDLINE-R and wavtool4vcv are also supported recommendations.

03

Beginning

イーライ is the original recording identity from which the later generations grew. The voice began from a more masculine position, but the project was never intended to freeze that first form as a final answer.

As the real voice provider's understanding of self-expression developed, the character and voice were allowed to develop with it. The later 茜音イーライ generation became softer and more expressive, and 暁 continues that movement further.

The beginning is part of the journey.

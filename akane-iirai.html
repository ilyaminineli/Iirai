03

Voice & expression

Compared with the original イーライ, 茜音イーライ has improved phoneme clarity, a clearer and more confident upper range and a refined expressive workflow. The core remains warm and bass-leaning, while the overall presentation has become softer and less tied to the original masculine position.

The character is intentionally allowed to move between familiar forms rather than settling into one fixed presentation. This follows the real voice provider's own growing understanding of himself and his search for a place between conventional gender roles.

04

Character

茜音イーライ is a scruffy, bright-eyed tinkerer and trickster who treats reality like an editable project file. Oversized scissors, tools, patched garments and mechanical details define his visual language.

He is hyperactive, talkative and playful, with fast rambling speech, glitched laughter and an affection for unfinished things. He is known as 怪人ハサミ — the “Scissor Kaijin” who cuts awkward silences and bad takes into pieces.

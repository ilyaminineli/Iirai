The voice keeps becoming itself

The first イーライ emerged from a more masculine vocal and visual origin. 茜音イーライ developed toward a more feminine, queer and gender-nonconforming expression. 暁 continues that trajectory with a broader expressive palette, new appends and extras and a new design.

The real voice provider, Ilya Minin (Eli), identifies as non-binary and is finding a place between genders. The changes in the voicebank are part of that artistic self-discovery rather than a fixed character reset.
